How I work

Understand

I start by breaking the problem into small pieces — what is needed, who the audience is, and which constraints count. Clear goals before the first line of code.

Design

Then I sketch and prototype in Figma. Iterating on a mockup is cheaper, faster and easier to compare.

Implement

Then I build an MVP, test, keep refining and add new features. Clean code, version control and planning are what carry it to long-term success.
